# ELIT100 Practice Midterm 1: Answer Key

Yazma sorularında (11, 14, 16) tek doğru cevap yok. Model cevaplar bir örnek; kendi cevabını puanlama ölçütüyle karşılaştır ya da sohbete yapıştır, geri bildirim vereyim.

## Part A: Note-taking

**1.** (Kendi sembollerin farklı olabilir; önemli olan kısa ve ilişkiyi gösteren biçim.)
a) `phone use in lectures → ↓ memory of content`
b) `library closed Sun. ∴ sts study in cafés` (ya da `∵ library closed Sun. → sts study in cafés`)
c) `online courses ≠ face-to-face (motivation)`
d) `* no. of int'l sts ↑, e.g. engineering depts`

**2.** Notes column (sağ, büyük alan): ders sırasında alınan notlar. Cue column (sol): anahtar kelimeler ve sorular, dersten sonra eklenir. Summary (alt): sayfanın birkaç cümlelik özeti.

**3.** Model: *Note-taking is a skill that develops progressively, and its aim is not to write faster but to identify what is worth writing down. A good note-taker listens actively, selects only the key ideas that are relevant to the purpose, and reviews the notes afterwards. Signpost expressions such as "the important thing here" help to decide what to write.*

## Part B: Subjective vs. objective language

**4.** a) O · b) F-NP · c) SSD · d) O · e) F-NP · f) SSD

Neden: a ve d yargı içeriyor (*too much, the worst*). b ve e genel gerçek, kanıt ister. c sayı, tarih ve kaynak veriyor; f bir uzmandan alıntı.

**5.** (Başka doğru cevaplar da olabilir; teknik adını yazman gerekiyordu.)
a) *Online exams may be unfair to some students.* (avoiding personal pronoun + hedging: modal, quantifier; *always* kaldırıldı)
b) *The researchers discovered that sleep deprivation has a significant effect on memory.* (phrasal verb: *found out → discovered*; emotive: *huge, terrible → significant*)
c) *Forty teachers were interviewed in March 2025.* (passive voice)
d) *Smith's (2020) study suggests that homework may have limited benefits.* (removing judgmental/emotive language: *amazing, proves, useless*; hedging)
e) *Video games may increase aggressive behaviour in some teenagers.* (hedging: modal *may*, quantifier *some*)
f) *These data indicate that the results are reliable.* (moving information around; *I believe* kaldırıldı)

**6.** Herhangi ikisi: *Obviously* (judgment / assumption), *ALL* (capitalisation, exaggeration), *completely*, *incredibly* (emotive, exaggeration), *!!* (exclamation marks).

## Part C: Paragraph

**7.** a) F · b) B · c) TS · d) F · e) TS

**8.** a) TS · b) S · c) S · d) U

**9.** *Many universities in Europe were founded in the Middle Ages.* Bu cümle egzersizin öğrencilere faydasından bahsetmiyor, topic sentence'ı desteklemiyor.

**10.** (1) For example · (2) because · (3) In addition · (4) such as · (5) However · (6) as a result

**11.** Model paragraph (139 words):

> The first year at university presents students with several challenges. First of all, many first-year students find it difficult to manage their time, as university courses require more independent study than high school. In addition, students who move to a new city often experience homesickness, which may affect their motivation and concentration. Financial responsibilities can also be demanding, since some students have to pay rent and bills for the first time. Furthermore, academic expectations are usually higher; for example, assignments must be written in a formal style and supported by reliable sources. As a result, a number of students receive lower grades in their first semester than they expected. Although these difficulties are common, they are generally temporary, and most students adapt once they develop effective study habits and support networks.

Kontrol listesi: TS = *the first year at university* (topic) + *presents several challenges* (controlling idea). Dört destek (zaman, ev özlemi, para, akademik beklenti). Transitions: *First of all, In addition, also, Furthermore, for example, As a result, Although.* *I / you* yok. Hedging: *many, often, may, some, usually, a number of, generally*. Concluding sentence ana fikri hatırlatıyor, *In conclusion* ile başlamıyor, yeni bir konu açmıyor.

## Part D: Formal register and email writing

**12.**
a) *Thank you for your email.*
b) *I apologise for not being able to attend class.*
c) *Please find attached my essay.*
d) *Could you please let me know your office hours?* / *I would be grateful if you could inform me of your office hours.*
e) *Please do not hesitate to contact me should you require any further information.*

**13.** faithfully; sincerely

**14.** Model email (121 words without the subject line):

> **Subject:** Absence from classes on 24 and 26 November: ELIT100, Section 3
>
> Dear Professor Kovač,
>
> I am Kaan [Surname], a student in your ELIT100 course, Section 3.
>
> I am writing to inform you that I will not be able to attend the classes on Tuesday and Thursday next week. I will be travelling to Vienna with the university's Engineering Club to attend a three-day international student conference on renewable energy. During the conference, I will take part in workshops and visit the Vienna University of Technology. This trip is closely related to my engineering programme, and I believe it will help me develop my academic communication skills.
>
> I would be grateful if you could let me know how I can make up for the missed classes and whether there are any materials I should study.
>
> Thank you for your understanding.
>
> Kind regards,
> Kaan [Surname]
> Student ID: [number]

Kontrol: konu satırı, *Dear Professor + soyad*, kim olduğunu söylüyor, amaç ilk cümlede, nereye / neden / ne kadar / bölümle ilgisi var, açık talep (*I would be grateful if you could...*), kısaltma yok, kibar kapanış. Tarihleri kendi takvimine göre değiştir.

## Part E: Summarising and paraphrasing

**15.** (Yapı ve kelime değişmeli; kaynak sonda.)
a) *Handwriting notes, rather than typing them, appears to help students retain more information (Patel, 2021).*
b) *A steep increase in city-centre rents has led many students to move to the suburbs (Morris & Lee, 2023).*
c) *Roughly one in four first-year students switch to a different major before their second year ends (Huang et al., 2019).*

Teknikler: a) synonym (*remember → retain*), yapı değişimi (özne değişti). b) neden-sonuç yer değiştirdi, *because → has led... to*. c) yüzde biçimi değişti (*25 percent → one in four*), synonym (*change → switch*).

**16.** Model summary (98 words):

> In an article published in *Popular Science* in 2017, Sara Chodosh questions headlines claiming that sleeping late at weekends causes heart disease. She explains that the study behind these claims found only a correlation: each hour by which people shift their weekend sleep schedule is linked to an 11 percent higher risk. Chodosh argues that other factors, such as heavy drinking, smoking or a poor diet, may explain this link. She points out that the real reason to keep a regular wake-up time is that irregular sleep makes people more tired, and she emphasises that an 11 percent increase is relatively small.

Kontrol: yazar adı + reporting verbs (*questions, explains, argues, points out, emphasises*), present simple, kendi kelimeler, sunburn ve sigara yüzdeleri gibi gereksiz detay yok, kendi görüşün yok.

## Part F: Plagiarism and APA

**17.**
a) **Yes.** Tırnak var ama kaynak yok. Tırnak tek başına yetmez.
b) **Yes.** Kısmi paraphrase: birkaç kelime değişmiş, yapı aynı. Kaynak olsa bile plagiarism (library metni Version 3, motivation e.g. 5).
c) **No.** Common knowledge, genel bilinen bir gerçek.
d) **Yes.** Aynı ödevi iki ders için teslim etmek (self-plagiarism).
e) **No.** Gerçek paraphrase ve kaynak var (library metni Version 5).

**18.**
a) *(Johnson & Smith, 2019)* — parantez içinde *&*.
b) *Brown and Davis (2021) argue that...* — cümle içinde *and*.
c) *(Martin et al., 2020)* — üç ve daha fazla yazarda ilk atıftan itibaren *et al.*
d) *"Language shapes thought" (Wilson, 2018, p. 12).* — direct quote sayfa no ister.
e) *(UNICEF, 2023)* — kurum adı resmi yazımıyla (büyük harf), yazar ile yıl arasında virgül.

**19.**
a) *et alia*, "and others". İtalik yazılmaz, *al.* noktalı.
b) 40 kelime ve üstü.
c) Herhangi ikisi: tırnak işareti yok; soldan 0.5 inç (1.27 cm) girinti; çift satır aralığı; nokta alıntının sonunda ve kaynaktan önce; yazar, yıl ve sayfa numarası verilir.
d) *(Johnson, n.d.)* — n.d. = no date.
e) Hayır. *The most talented* bir görüş; common knowledge sadece fact'leri kapsar, görüşler kaynak ister (Bach örneği).
